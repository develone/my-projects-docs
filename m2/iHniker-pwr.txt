These are results for what pin on raspberry pi 5 used with iUniker NVMe 
power plug

The 2-pin auxiliary power cable included with the iUniker NVMe HAT 
connects to the 5V Power and Ground (GND) pins on the Raspberry Pi 5's 
40-pin GPIO header.

5V Power (+5V): Pin 2 or Pin 4

Ground (GND): Pin 6, 9, 14, 20, 25, 30, 34, or 39 (Pin 6 or 14 are 
commonly used next to the 5V pins

Note: Double-check the polarity markings (silk screen) on your 
specific iUniker NVMe expansion board before plugging the cable in to 
avoid reversing 5V and Ground.
